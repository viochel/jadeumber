A FEW DETAILS

Before the
first cup.

What is in your core collection?+

Our core collection focuses on first and second harvest Japanese matcha and premium hojicha. Additional grades at other price points are available upon request.

Can we evaluate samples first?+

Yes. We can discuss a sample selection based on your intended application. Sample availability, quantities, and any shipping or handling charges are confirmed before arrangements are made.

What are your minimum orders and lead times?+

Minimum order quantities, pricing, and lead times are quoted for the specific selection, order size, and destination. Share your requirements so these details can be confirmed for your project.

Which matcha is right for our menu?+

Start with how you plan to serve it: straight, with milk, or in a recipe. We can help you compare selections through sampling. The individual tea and your preparation matter alongside the harvest.
