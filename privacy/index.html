INFORMATION / JADE UMBER

Privacy Policy

Clear information for this website and its current preview experience.

Preview policy · Updated September 26, 2026. This notice describes the current website preview. Business contact details and commerce terms must be finalized before public sales begin.

Scope of this notice

This website presents the Jade Umber collection and gifting concepts. This preview does not accept orders, process payments, or transmit inquiry-form submissions to Jade Umber.

Information entered in the inquiry form

Names, email addresses, business details, and messages you enter are used in your browser to prepare a brief. The form does not send those details to Jade Umber and does not deliberately save them in browser storage. You control whether to download, copy, or share the brief. Browser autofill and downloaded files are controlled by your browser and device.

Gift bag and local storage

When you add gifts to your bag, this site saves the gift identifiers and quantities in local storage on your device so your planning list can persist between pages and visits. Clear the bag or use the clear-data control on our cookie and storage notice to remove it. No name, email, or payment details are stored with the bag.

Technical information and hosting

The hosting and access provider may process technical information such as IP address, request details, and access logs to deliver and protect the site. Private preview access may require authentication controlled by the hosting provider. These services have their own privacy practices.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking choices

The authored website does not install analytics pixels, advertising trackers, or third-party marketing scripts. It does not use its gift-bag data for cross-site tracking, sale, or targeted advertising. Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control settings do not change the authored site’s behavior because those tracking features are not present.

Future checkout and inquiry services

Before online checkout or inquiry delivery is enabled, this notice must be updated to describe the actual seller, contact channel, service providers, information uses, retention, and applicable privacy request process. Planned integration is not a statement that it is currently active.

Questions and changes

During the private preview, contact the person who shared this site with you with questions or concerns. A public privacy contact has not yet been designated. The updated date above identifies this notice version. This preview notice is not a completed policy for future online sales.
