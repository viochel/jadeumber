What is included in each gift?+

The four price tiers are set at $50, $125, $175, and $250 USD per gift. Specific tea selections, quantities, accessories, and packaging are still being finalized. Final contents will be confirmed before any purchase.

Can we create something above $250?+

Yes, share your budget and occasion for a bespoke gifting discussion. Contents, feasibility, minimum quantities, and timing will be confirmed in a proposal.

Can we request branding or multiple delivery addresses?+

Include those requirements in your brief. Personalization options, multi-address shipping, fees, and feasibility must be confirmed for your project; they are not included or guaranteed at this stage.

When should we place a holiday order?+

Share your desired arrival date and quantities early. No shipping deadline or delivery guarantee is published yet. An order-specific timeline must be confirmed before purchase.

Are taxes and shipping included?+

The displayed amounts are the gift tier prices in USD. Taxes, shipping, and any custom work will be confirmed separately. The budget calculator estimates gift value only.

Can we buy online now?+

The collection is in preview and checkout is not yet open. You can explore tiers, plan quantities, and prepare an inquiry brief. No payment is collected on this site.
